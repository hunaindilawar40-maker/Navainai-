FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about Navain AI.

How long does setup take? +
Our team gets your AI agent live within 24 hours of signing up. We handle all the configuration, scripting, and testing — you just provide some basic information about your business and we do the rest.
Will it work with my existing phone number? +
Yes! Navain AI works with virtually any business phone system including VoIP, landlines, and mobile numbers. We set up smart call forwarding so your existing number stays the same — callers won't notice any change.
Does it work for my industry? +
Navain AI works across virtually every business sector — healthcare, legal, automotive, real estate, food service, trades, retail, and more. Our agents are fully customized to understand your specific business, services, and terminology.
Will customers know they're talking to AI? +
Our AI agents sound incredibly natural and conversational. Most callers simply experience a smooth, professional interaction. You can choose to disclose AI usage or keep it transparent — we support both approaches based on your preference.
What happens to the calls and data? +
Every call is recorded, transcribed, and summarized. You receive full call logs and lead details after each interaction. All data is securely stored and accessible through your dashboard anytime.
How is pricing determined? +
Pricing is based on your monthly call volume and the features you need. We offer flexible plans for small businesses all the way to enterprise scale. Contact us for a custom quote tailored to your business.
